# Repository guidance

This is the experimental personal website for Adam Jones, a JavaScript developer and designer.

- Keep the site static, lightweight, responsive, keyboard-friendly, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Prefer plain language and factual content supplied by Adam. Do not invent biography, achievements, employers, clients, or testimonials.
- Keep private information, credentials, factory records, and internal logs out of published assets.
- Do not publish, configure DNS, enable paid services, merge pull requests, or alter adamjones.ca.
- Avoid scroll hijacking, unnecessary animation libraries, and backend code without a concrete need.
