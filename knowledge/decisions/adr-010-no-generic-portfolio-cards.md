# ADR-10: Reject Generic Portfolio Cards and Gradients

Status: **confirmed**

## Context

Generic portfolio tropes (card grids, gradient backgrounds, corporate polish) would dilute the site's personality.

## Decision

Avoid generic portfolio cards, gradients, decorative clutter, sterile corporate polish, and arbitrary cosmetic churn. Favor concept-driven visual moments.

## Consequences

- Work section uses inline SVG studies and editorial layout instead of thumbnail cards.
- Visual interest comes from illustration, typography, and space rather than effects.
- Changes must earn their place by improving the concept, not just refreshing cosmetics.
