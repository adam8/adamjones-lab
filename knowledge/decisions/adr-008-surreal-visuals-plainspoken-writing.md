# ADR-8: Surreal Visuals, Plainspoken Writing

Status: **confirmed**

## Context

The site needs a distinctive voice: imaginative but credible.

## Decision

The creative direction pairs **surreal, concept-driven visuals** with **plainspoken, factual writing**. Identity and project claims are factual only.

## Consequences

- Copy avoids marketing hyperbole.
- Visuals are allowed to be surprising and eccentric if they remain beautifully executed.
- Bio, client lists, outcomes, and testimonials are not invented.
