# Design Principles

Status: **confirmed**

Source: [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md), [design/DECISIONS.md](../../design/DECISIONS.md)

## TL;DR

The site's creative direction is set by Adam and captured in `design/TASTE.md`. Reviews are advisory; only explicit human decisions authorize changes.

## Core principles

1. **Curiosity, not announcement.** The portfolio should make visitors feel curious, rather than announce curiosity. [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md)
2. **Surreal visuals, plainspoken writing.** Imaginative, surprising visuals paired with factual, plain text. [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md)
3. **Factual identity only.** Do not invent biography, achievements, employers, clients, or testimonials. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)
4. **Concept-driven moments.** Memorable, concept-driven visual moments earn their place. Restraint can strengthen originality. [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md)
5. **Protect typography and motion safeguards.** Excellent typography, legible hierarchy, deliberate space, and coherent visual language are non-negotiable. [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md)
6. **Native scrolling only.** No scroll hijacking, bounded and reversible motion, keyboard access, visible focus, readable fallbacks. [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md)
7. **No reduced-motion mode.** Adam explicitly removed dedicated `prefers-reduced-motion` behavior. Other safeguards remain. [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md)
8. **Keep success unchanged.** A successful design should not be altered unless a high-impact intervention earns its risk. [design/TASTE.md](../../design/TASTE.md)

## Anti-patterns

- Generic portfolio cards and gradients
- Decorative clutter
- Sterile corporate polish
- Arbitrary cosmetic churn
- Compensating for a failed experiment by piling on more effects
